GK Philosophy — The Four Phases
Phase 1
In Possession
The GK is the first attacker — not a spectator. Once the ball is secured, the mentality shifts immediately to attack. The first pass dictates how the team moves forward.

• Play away from pressure, never into it
• Build wide first — play to the wing, wing carries, then combine
• If pressing trap is on wide → play to the dropping center mid
• As the ball moves forward, the GK advances with it — stays available for drops and weak-side switches
• Footwork must be there to execute — it's the foundation of everything in possession
"The pass you make dictates how we move forward."
Phase 2
Attacking → Defending (A to D)
The moment possession is lost, recover to goal immediately. The GK should not be sitting on the 6-yard line — they are pushed up. Recovery is the first response.

• Recover to defending position — no hesitation
• Simultaneously organize and align the back line while dropping
• Get the back four in correct shape and funnel together
• The GK and the fullback must be in sync — they are the two biggest communicators on the team
"Not only drops into defending position — gets the defense in line."
Phase 3
Out of Possession
Correct positioning stops the first shot. The GK must be in the right place before the shot is taken — not reacting after.

• Near post is already covered — far post is the priority concern
• When ball is central: push off the goal line and cut down angles
• When ball is wide: move in line with ball, prepare for near-post shot and far-post cross
• All saves aren't catches — get it out and away when necessary
• Far post footwork drills are non-negotiable for this reason
"Correct positioning stops the first shot."
Phase 4
Defending → Attacking (D to A)
When the ball is won — carry it as long as possible. A GK pressing forward forces a defender to press them, which creates an overload and a numerical advantage for the team.

• Ball secured → carry forward → force a press → play into the overload
• 10v10 becomes 9v10 for the opponent the moment the GK draws that press
• Goal kick progression: wide first → dropping mid → long option to the forward making a run
• GK reads the game emotionally — if one option keeps failing, adjust to the next
• Confidence in footwork + decision-making is what makes this work
"Carry the ball — force them to press you, create the overload."

Session Library by Block
MD-4 · Block 1 · High Volume
Handling Foundation + The Box
Warm-Up (15 min)
The Box footwork series — 3×3×10-yd grid. Side to side (feet and hands). Move forward to side panels. Forward to front panel. Pass back to coach between sets. 3 sets / 6–8 reps per set.
Main Theme (25 min)
Handling in the goal — progress side to side from 12 yards. Head height, scoop below waist, waist height, overhead. Then side to side using full goal width. Forward/backward movement in the 6-yard box. 4–6 serves each position. One keeper works, one stretches.
Serving Areas
Penalty spot · Off the angle (12 yds, 4 yds outside) · Edge of the D · 6–8 reps each area
"First attacker mentality — build the habits that make the first pass reliable."
MD-3 · Block 1 · Moderate Load
Distribution — First Attacker in Action
Warm-Up (15 min)
18-yard channel footwork (3 yds wide). Jog outside / backpedal inside. Slalom cone to cone. Touch low, leap high on opposite side (2×6). Coach moves backward serving as keeper works forward. Save high / save low combos.
Main Theme (25 min)
Central: Receive (catch or feet), open with 1st touch, dribble to corner of 18-yard area, recover back without ball. Pass to gates.

Side-on: Receive and open with 1st touch to play — short target (outside back), intermediate (outside mid), long (target). Work both kicks and throws.

Philosophy: First look always long to stretch opponents defensively. If wide option fails — play to the dropping center mid.
Game Integration
Folds into 8v8 or 6v6+4. GK focus: starting positions, communication with back four, interaction with team.
"Play away from pressure, not into it. The pass you make dictates how we move forward."
MD-4 · Block 2 · High Volume
Shot Handling + Cross/Shot Combos
Warm-Up (15 min)
Shot handling in goal — two servers 8 yards out, even with the posts. Slingshot serves to head/shoulder height. Bowled serves on floor (scooped save). One low / one volleyed, alternate sides. High ball — claim at crossbar height. Both volley: one set at posts / one set angled in. 3 sets / 6–8 reps per set.
Main Theme (25 min)
Cross/Shot Combos: One keeper on flank, coach in and around box. Keeper takes cross → distributes to determined side → takes shot from coach.

3 Service Areas: 30 yards · 15 yards · 5 yards
Targets: Front post · Central · Back post
Shot locations: Off opposite angle · Same-side angle · Inside/outside box

Rebounds are live. Always end with a save.
Coaching Points
Crosses: Assess ball early — attack ball late. Shots: Recovery movement → set position → recovery saves. Distribution: Look to throw to side the serve takes you — hit gaps behind server to set counter attack.
"Assess early, attack late. Far post is the biggest concern — near post is already covered."
MD-3 · Block 2 · Moderate Load
Crossing — Range + Decision-Making
Warm-Up (15 min)
The Gate — falling/collapsing focus (6-yard wide gate with coaching sticks). Various starting positions: on side, redirections. Serves: volleys, half-volleys, sidewinders. 3 sets / 6–8 reps per set.
Main Theme (25 min)
Increasing range in the goal — crosses:
Central: Side to side, width of posts. Side-on: Forward/backward from front post. Touch the post: Cutbacks from the end line — force collapse dive save.

6-Yard Box: Hand serves from edge of six — resistance from 2nd keeper.
18-Yard Box: Crosses from outside (5, 15, 30 yards). Inswinging and outswinging. Resistance from 2nd keeper. Throw back to opposite server. Coach at top of box — shot after cross is dealt with.
Game Integration
6v6+4 — 4 players in wide zones, goal must come from a crossed ball. GK focus: Protect near post, organize defenders at back post. Catch/punch/box decision on every cross.
"All saves aren't catches — get it out and away when needed."
MD-4 · Block 3 · High Volume
Distribution Under Pressure + D to A Transition
Warm-Up (15 min)
Handling in goal — various ball movement. Still ball. Moving towards keeper. Moving away from keeper. Moving across the keeper. Get in line with ball quickly. Key is balance in the set position.
Main Theme (25 min)
Distribution decisions under pressure:
When ahead — slow tempo, play short, retain possession.
When behind — look long first and fast. Furthest option first.

Carry the ball as long as possible to draw press → create overload. 10v10 becomes 9v10 the moment GK draws that press. Once the forward is drawn in — play into the advantage.
Game Integration
6v6v6 — angle play focus + D to A role switch. GK focus: Change of mode from defending to attacking. Change of mentality — instantaneous. Starting positions further off line.
"Carry the ball — force them to press you, create the overload."
MD-3 · Block 3 · Moderate Load
Angle Play + 1v1 Decision-Making
Warm-Up (15 min)
Ladder footwork — quick feet, side to side. Handling in the goal: volleys to hands, collapse diving from side, shots off the angles. K1 in goal, K2 in front with ball. K2 bowls to coach at top of box. Coach shoots one-time strike. Keepers rotate. Serving keeper starts passive → progresses to screen working keeper.
Main Theme (25 min)
1v1 angle play: Close shooters down — cut the angle before the shot. Set position before ball is struck. Recovery movement after every save.

Starting positions: Get higher off the line when ball is 30–40 yards away. Stay active and switched on. Be alive — all rebounds are live until secured.

4v4+6: Full goals, 20×40-yard area. GK focus: quickness and alertness to dangerous situations, 1v1s with shooters.
"Correct positioning stops the first shot. If in position to win it — win it."
MD-3 · Tournament Prep · Sharp + Controlled
Pre-Tournament Sharpening — Shot + Cross
Warm-Up (15 min)
2 min on your own — jog, stretch, knock a ball around. Shot handling: quick but balanced movement between saves. Two servers 8 yards out. Slingshot serves. One low / one high alternating. Quicken pace each set. End every set with a save — always finish on success.
Main Theme (20 min)
Best cross/shot combo routine from the season — the one that got the sharpest response. Run it clean. No new serves, no new progressions. Confidence only.

GK focus: Reading the ball. Starting positions. Catch/punch/box decision at full game speed.
No New Concepts Rule
Zero new information this week. Run what was built. Caseam's role: energy and confidence. The coaching is done — trust what was installed across Blocks 1–3.
"Tournament football — the press standard doesn't drop. It rises."
MD-1 · Match Day Eve · Full Team
Pre-Game Routine
Routine (25 min with team)
Jog and stretch with full group. Possession game — GK joins in (5v2s are great for touch, mobility, and relationship).

Then GK-specific: Ladder footwork → hands and feet → angle shots from penalty spot, off the angle, edge of D → shot handling (movement between saves: quick but balanced).

Adapt for conditions: wet surface → more skipping balls. Glare → address ball-reading early. Turf → distribution practice with pass backs.
Shadow Play + Set Pieces
Cross and finishing — three groups: one each flank, one central. Three-man pattern with cross. Movement in box: front post, back post, penalty spot. GK focus: stay/go decision on corners. Organize the wall.
"One voice in the box. GK and fullback in sync — those two must be in cahoots."
Coach P Toolkit — Favorite Routines
ROUTINE 01
The Box
3×3×10-yd grid · 2 keepers · 8–10 balls
Footwork focus. Starting at back panel with a pass back to coach.

Progression: Side to side (feet and hands) → move forward to side panels → forward from outside to front panels → same sequence with pass to 2nd keeper as target.

3 sets each / 6–8 reps per set. Add falling/collapsing: start on side, on coach's call get to feet and step through front panel to save. Progress to two saves (dead ball + volley). 4 reps / 2 saves each.
ROUTINE 02
K1/K2 Shot Rotation
Full goal · coach at top of box · 8–10 balls
K1 in goal, K2 in front with ball.

K2 bowls to coach at top of box. Coach shoots one-time strike at goal. Keepers rotate — K1 bowls to coach. Serving keeper starts passive → progresses to screen working keeper.

Progression: K1 in goal with ball, K2 in front. K1 bowls through K2's legs. One-touch shot from coach — K2 saves. Progress to same but K2 facing K1 on serve, then turns to save.

6–10 saves / 2 sets each. All rebounds live.
ROUTINE 03
18-Yard Channel Footwork
18-yd long channel · 3 yds wide · cones
Jog outside, backpedal inside (2×4). Slalom cone to cone (1×6). Slalom touching cones with hands — sharper (1×6). Touch low, leap high on opposite side (2×6). Touch low, catch high ball (2×6). Coach moves backward serving as keeper works forward (2×6). Leap high, fall on low ball (2×6). Save high/save low (2×6). Save low/save high (2×6).

Stretch with medicine ball between sets. This is a warm-up with purposeful build-up.
ROUTINE 04
Cross/Shot Combo
Full goal · flank server · coach at D · 3 service areas
One keeper on flank. Coach in and around the box. Keeper takes cross → distributes to determined side → gets in line with coach → takes shot from coach.

3 Service Areas: 30 yards · 15 yards · 5 yards from end line
Targets: Front post · Central · Back post
Shot locations: Off opposite angle · Same-side angle · In/outside box

Variation: Coach chooses not to shoot — observe for drop in form or fatigue. 3–5 crosses each area. Change sides.
ROUTINE 05
Four Man Back Pass Rotation
18-yd area · 2 full goals · 8–10 balls
Keepers work in pairs — 2 keepers at either side of the 18-yard area, 2 inside about 5–7 yards away. Ball starts with long ball to opposite side. Pair of keepers exchange double pass, then ball sent long to other side. Keepers change position when ball is on the other side.

Options long ball: All throwing · All feet · Combo
Options exchange: All feet · All catching/throwing · Catch long ball, bowl to inside keeper for one-time shot

Sharpens footwork and distribution under movement pressure.
ROUTINE 06
Increase Range — Front Post
Full goal · coach at side edge of 6-yd box · 8–10 balls
Gets feet moving and builds confidence commanding the 18-yard area.

Keeper starts middle of goal: Low ball for keeper to scoop.
Keeper at front post: Looping serve — keeper backpedals and catches overhead.
Keeper top of six at front post: Move down to touch near post → dive for low ball back across goal.

Optional: Add second shooter at top of box to increase difficulty. This routine directly trains far post footwork — the priority area in Caseam's philosophy.
